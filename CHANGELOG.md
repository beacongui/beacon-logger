# Changelog

Versions follow the releases archived on Zenodo.

## v2.0.0 · 13 May 2026

DOI [10.5281/zenodo.20144661](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20144661)

- Added dataset and paper citations to the documentation.
- Added a research use disclaimer.
- Published a prebuilt Windows binary, `logger.exe`, on Zenodo.
- Licence changed to CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.
- `logger.py` is unchanged from v1.0.0.

## v1.0.0 · 7 May 2026

DOI [10.5281/zenodo.20062628](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20062628)

- First public release of the logger used to collect the BEACON dataset.
- Synchronised capture of keystrokes, mouse kinematics, network traffic, screen video,
  hardware metadata and VALORANT configuration, with a built-in consent step.
- Released under CC BY 4.0.
